FieldWindow

Turn live weather forecasts and explicit operating thresholds into explainable, continuous work windows for drone and outdoor field operations.

Ready. Demo presets are transparent heuristics, not authoritative operating limits.
Decision-support only. FieldWindow does not replace legal, aviation, safety, manufacturer, pesticide-label, airspace/NOTAM, terrain/obstacle, or on-site requirements. Weather data via Open-Meteo for hackathon/non-commercial prototype use.
